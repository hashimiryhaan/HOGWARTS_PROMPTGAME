## 📜 The Legend Begins
**Shadows of Hogwarts** is a fully interactive, terminal-based Role-Playing Game that puts you at the center of your own wizarding story. 

Forget pressing buttons on a controller—your survival depends on your choices, your memory, and a little bit of luck. From the dusty shelves of Ollivanders to a deadly, spell-slinging climax in a darkened graveyard, every decision shapes your destiny.

---

### 💀 THE DARK LORD CHALLENGE
In the end , you will face Lord Voldemort in a randomized, turn-based duel. 
* **Tactical Combat:** Balance offensive strikes (*Reducto*) and stunning tactics (*Expecto Patronum*).
* **The Unforgivable Curse:** Risk it all on *Avada Kedavra*—an attack with a massive 85% fail rate, but an instant-kill if it lands.
* **Dynamic :** Voldemort fights back with randomized Dark Arts spells, ensuring no two playthroughs are ever the same.
